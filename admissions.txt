Rayners College London — Admissions Policy

Rayners College London (RCL) welcomes applications from UK home students and international students across all seven of our courses: Business Management, Computer Science, Computing Technologies, Cyber Security, Psychology, Data Science, and Healthcare.

Entry requirements vary by course and by partner university, but as a general guide, applicants should hold a minimum of five GCSEs (or equivalent) including English and Mathematics, plus relevant Level 3 qualifications (A-Levels, BTEC, or an Access to Higher Education Diploma) for undergraduate entry.

Applications are accepted year-round on a rolling basis across our three campuses: London, Birmingham, and Manchester. There is no fixed application deadline, but early application is recommended to secure a place, particularly for the January and September intakes.

The application process is:
1. Submit an enquiry via our website or WhatsApp.
2. Attend a course consultation with an advisor.
3. Submit supporting documents (proof of qualifications, ID, and, for international students, proof of English proficiency).
4. Receive a conditional or unconditional offer.
5. Confirm your place with a deposit.

For questions about admissions, contact the RCL team via WhatsApp or through the enquiry form on raynerscollege.co.uk.
