Rayners College London — Partner Universities

Rayners College London (RCL) is a UK higher education consultancy partnered with three universities, each offering degree pathways across our seven courses:

1. Southampton Solent University — known for strong industry links and practical, career-focused teaching. Popular for Business Management and Computing Technologies pathways.

2. London Metropolitan University — offers flexible entry routes and a strong focus on employability, with popular pathways in Psychology and Healthcare.

3. QA — a leading provider of technology and digital skills training, offering accelerated, industry-aligned routes into Computer Science, Cyber Security, and Data Science.

Students studying through RCL are taught at one of our three campuses — London, Birmingham, or Manchester — while working towards a degree awarded by one of these partner universities. Entry requirements and course structures depend on which partner university and course a student chooses, and RCL advisors help match each applicant to the most suitable pathway.
